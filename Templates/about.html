About the Minnesota Wild

The Minnesota Wild are a professional ice hockey team based in Saint Paul, Minnesota. Founded in 1997 and beginning play in the 2000–01 season, the Wild compete in the National Hockey League (NHL) as a member of the Central Division in the Western Conference, playing their home games at Xcel Energy Center.
